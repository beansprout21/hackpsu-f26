# FNB (First National Bank / F.N.B. Corporation) Consumer Product Catalog

Researched and verified: 2026-10-24. Every product fact comes from an official fnb-online.com page, linked under each product.

## Assumptions and caveats

- **Rates are gated by ZIP code.** Almost every FNB deposit and loan page says "enter a Zip Code to view rates", so APYs and APRs do not appear in the page text. They are marked "not published" unless a figure appears on the page itself. The only exceptions found are the CD Special APYs and the SmartRate card APR.
- **"Not published" means the figure was not on the cited page.** The figure may still appear in a linked PDF (pricing information, fee schedule, or account disclosures). Those PDFs were not read.
- **Online and in-branch opening deposits differ.** Many accounts take $0 to open in a branch and $25 to $50 to open online.
- **Fee amounts are often missing.** Several checking pages state the conditions that waive a monthly fee but not the fee amount.
- Allowed goal tags: first_home, emergency_fund, travel, small_business, education, family, credit_building, everyday_banking.

## 1. Checking

### Student Checking
- **Category:** checking (student)
- **For:** high school, college, trade school and graduate students.
  - The student must be the applicant. Applicants under 18 need a joint account with a parent or guardian.
  - Domestic students need ID plus their school name.
  - International students need a passport or Permanent Resident Card plus a visa. Without an SSN or ITIN they must open in person, with two forms of ID and a W-8BEN.
- **Monthly fee:** none ("No monthly balance or service charge"). Paper statements may carry a fee, which e-statements avoid.
- **Minimum opening deposit:** not published
- **Minimum balance:** none
- **APY:** not published
- **Overdraft:** "No overdraft fees". FNB declines or returns transactions when funds are short.
- **Other features:**
  - FNB refunds its own fee for using non-FNB ATMs, up to 4 times per statement cycle.
  - One free incoming wire per statement cycle.
  - CardGuard, Zelle, mobile deposit, Payment Switch and Credit Center.
  - Entry into the $15,000 scholarship promotion.
- **Goals:** everyday_banking, education, credit_building
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.fnb-online.com/personal/checking-savings/checking/student-checking
  - https://www.fnb-online.com/landing/international-students
- **Verified:** 2026-10-24

### eStyle Checking
- **Category:** checking (checkless, no-fee-eligible)
- **For:** anyone with a non-expired ID. The account is Bank On certified.
- **Monthly fee:** amount not published. It is waived with 1 or more deposits, or 5 or more customer-initiated transactions, per statement cycle.
- **Minimum opening deposit:** $25 online; $0 in branch
- **Minimum balance:** not published
- **APY:** not published (none stated)
- **Overdraft:** no overdraft fee. Transactions are declined or returned.
- **Other features:** no paper checks; free Visa debit card; Zelle; RTP/FedNow instant payments.
- **Goals:** everyday_banking, credit_building
- **Source:** https://fnb-online.com/Personal/Checking-Savings/Checking/eStyle-Checking
- **Verified:** 2026-10-24

### eStyle Plus Checking
- **Category:** checking (checkless, interest-bearing)
- **For:** anyone with a non-expired ID
- **Monthly fee:** amount not published. It is waived with either:
  - a $1,000 daily balance in the account, or
  - $25,000 combined daily balance across FNB accounts in the preceding month.
- **Minimum opening deposit:** $50 online; $0 in branch
- **Minimum balance:** not published
- **APY:** not published. Interest is earned on balances of $0–$2,500.
- **Overdraft:** no overdraft fee. Transactions are declined or returned.
- **Other features:**
  - FNB refunds its own fee for using non-FNB ATMs, up to 4 times per cycle.
  - FNB refunds fees other banks charge, up to $12 per cycle.
- **Goals:** everyday_banking, emergency_fund
- **Source:** https://www.fnb-online.com/personal/checking-savings/checking/estyle-plus-checking
- **Verified:** 2026-10-24

### Freestyle Checking

- **For:** anyone with a non-expired ID

### Premierstyle Checking
- **Category:** checking (premium, interest-bearing)
- **For:** anyone with a non-expired ID

  - a $7,500 daily balance, or

- **Verified:** 2026-10-24

- No overdraft fee on transactions of $5 or less.

- Overdraft and NSF fee amounts were reduced, but the new amounts are not published.

## 2. Savings and money market

### FirstRate Savings
- **Category:** savings
- **For:** anyone with a non-expired ID. FNB pitches it as the companion account to Student Checking.
- **Monthly fee:** $5, waived with an average daily balance of $500 or more
- **Minimum opening deposit:** $50 online; $0 in branch
- **Minimum balance:** $500 average daily balance to avoid the fee
- **APY:** not published (tiered rate; interest compounds quarterly)
- **Limits:** 6 transfers and withdrawals per month; $15 for each one over the limit. Branch and ATM transactions are unlimited.
- **Goals:** emergency_fund, travel, first_home, family
- **Source:** https://www.fnb-online.com/personal/checking-savings/savings/firstrate-savings
- **Verified:** 2026-10-24

### FirstRate Money Market

- **For:** anyone with a non-expired ID
